# SENTEC Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Signal / Structure

Very dark editorial engineering interface with a warm signal orange, calibrated grids, and an exact supplied logo that activates like a working system.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Field Notes

Light scientific-journal aesthetic with blueprint blue, orange annotations, and tactile diagram fragments.

**Probability:** 0.03

### Theme Name: Orbit / Pulse

Atmospheric midnight interface with luminous technical fields and a cinematic animated mark.

**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen approach: Signal / Structure

### Design Movement

Contemporary Swiss-influenced editorial design fused with industrial control-panel graphics and kinetic identity systems.

### Core Principles

1. The exact supplied SENTEC artwork is the hero’s source of truth; animate it through registered layers rather than reconstructing it.
2. Use asymmetry and editorial pacing rather than generic centered page compositions.
3. Pair high-contrast ink-black surfaces with one ownable orange signal color and quiet steel-blue support tones.
4. Let engineering details become navigation cues through ticks, brackets, status labels, coordinates, and measured spacing.

### Color Philosophy

Near-black creates focus and seriousness in dark mode, while signal orange (`#F15A24`) behaves as an active indicator rather than decoration. Cool steel and muted blue-gray tones provide technical depth without competing with the brand mark. The light interpretation uses a clinical sky-blue drafting plane with deep navy structural contrast and preserves the same orange signal.

### Layout Paradigm

The home page uses an editorial split: a narrative copy rail on the left and a large **unframed** animated SENTEC artwork on the right. Subsequent sections use offset columns, slim rails, and card clusters with deliberate empty space rather than repetitive centered blocks.

### Signature Elements

- A kinetic, exact SENTEC mark with source-aligned detail highlights and a restrained orange calibration sweep.
- Thin measurement ticks, corner brackets, and monospaced system labels.
- A persistent signal-line motif that connects the hero to content sections.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel instrument-like: hover states brighten a detail, buttons compress slightly, and transitions are precise and quiet. The hero’s motion comes from the supplied artwork’s aligned layers, not from newly drawn decorative geometry.

### Animation

The hero enters through a staged source-artwork activation: the base establishes the full logo, selected upper details and the core receive aligned highlights, a signal sweep crosses the composition, and the wordmark resolves. Afterward, motion remains restrained. All nonessential animation pauses under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System

Use Space Grotesk for expressive headings and IBM Plex Mono for metadata, labels, and navigation accents. Body copy uses a clean system sans stack for readability. Headlines are tight and weighty; technical labels are uppercase, tracked, and small.

### Brand Essence

SENTEC is the student engineering society that turns curiosity into capability through real projects, shared practice, and visible impact.

**Personality:** exacting, kinetic, generous.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are concise and declarative. Calls to action feel like invitations into a working system rather than generic conversion prompts.

> “Build what the brief leaves out.”

> “Enter the signal.”

### Wordmark & Logo

Use the supplied original artwork exactly as provided. The hero keeps its native portrait composition, including the illustrated mark, SENTEC wordmark, and tagline. It is deliberately **unframed**: do not add a square field, circular halo, separate orbit ring, or other backing geometry behind the logo unless the design direction is explicitly revised.

### Signature Brand Color

Signal Orange — `#F15A24`.

## Style Decisions

- Dark is the default theme. Light mode is a clinical drafting-table interpretation, not a generic white reskin; retain cool sky-blue surfaces, deep navy panels and type, orange signals, texture, and the full public navigation.
- Every route carries one structural calibration cue: an orange signal line, measured tick strip, bracket, or coordinate label.
- Portraits and visual archive cards use dark technical overlays, restrained orange metadata, and deliberate crops so external media remains inside the SENTEC system.
- The homepage hero uses the exact supplied logo over its technical texture only. The previous added square/circular backing module is retired and must not be restored accidentally.
